The Journey

From a tea vendor at the railway platforms of Kishanganj, Bihar — to stewarding heritage estates and modern lifestyle brands across India.

The journey has never lost its humility, nor its hunger.

My early days were as a tea vendor, however, the keen observer within me felt the connection of tea with people and how it dramatically changed their day or mood. I was determined to learn more about tea and strengthen this connection.

The journey in the tea industry — of becoming an entrepreneur, of launching a distinguishable difference in the culture of tea-drinking — is filled with a bold and courageous risk appetite.
